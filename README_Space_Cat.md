# Space Cat: Build a Spaceship 🐱🚀

**Team Paws4Dev | NASA Space Apps Challenge — Space Mission Design Game**

[Play the game](https://pawstronaut.casa)

## The mission

After crash-landing on Mars, a space cat is stranded millions of miles from Earth. The cat is carrying valuable scientific discoveries gathered across the galaxy, and both the explorer and the data need a way home.

Your mission is to design a spacecraft, choose its parts, and launch. How will your choices affect the rocket’s performance—and will your design get the cat closer to Earth?

## How to play

1. Choose the components for your spacecraft, including an engine, fuel tank, body, and fins.
2. Consider how your choices affect the rocket’s mass, fuel, thrust, and stability.
3. Launch your design and observe what happens.
4. Adjust your choices and try again.

A stronger engine can provide more thrust, while additional fuel adds weight. Players can experiment with different combinations and learn from each attempt.

## Learning through experimentation

The game introduces space mission engineering through an approachable, simplified physics model. Players can explore relationships between:

- **Thrust and gravity:** Can the rocket lift off?
- **Fuel and mass:** Can it carry enough fuel without becoming too heavy?
- **Air resistance and stability:** How do the atmosphere and the spacecraft’s design affect flight?

Rather than relying on one perfect answer, players can test ideas, notice results, and revise their designs. The game is intended for curious players of different ages who enjoy space, building, and discovery.
